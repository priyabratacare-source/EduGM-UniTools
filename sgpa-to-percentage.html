Frequently asked questions
Which formula should I use?

Check your university's official handbook or marksheet. If unsure, pick "Auto" — we'll show you every common formula so you can compare. MAKAUT and AKTU students usually use (SGPA − 0.75) × 10, while VTU and KTU use SGPA × 10.

What is the maximum percentage I can get?

With SGPA 10 and the (SGPA − 0.75) × 10 formula, the result is 92.5%. With SGPA × 10, it's 100%. Some universities cap the result at 100% regardless of formula.

Is this SGPA or CGPA?

This tool works for both — SGPA (semester) and CGPA (cumulative) use the same conversion formulas at most institutions. Just enter whichever value you have.

My university uses a 4-point scale. Does this work?

Yes, but the result will only be meaningful if your university's own conversion rule is applied. Select "4-point scale" to scale the input proportionally — but always verify with your official conversion chart.

Does this tool store my data?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your SGPA is never sent to a server or saved after you close the page.
