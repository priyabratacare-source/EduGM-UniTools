Common Engineering Formulas

Ohm's Law Voltage: V = I × R
V = voltage, I = current, R = resistance
Electrical Power P = V × I
P = power, V = voltage, I = current
Density ρ = m / V
Mass divided by volume
Velocity v = distance / time
For average speed along a path
Mechanical Stress σ = F / A
Force divided by cross-sectional area
Mechanical Strain ε = ΔL / L
Change in length divided by original length
Rectangle Area A = length × width
Circle Area A = πr²
r = radius

Check units and applicable assumptions before using a formula in an engineering design.
