Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to EduGM UniTools.

🔒 All PDF & academic tools run locally in your browser

Last Updated: November 14, 2026 · Updated by: EduGM UniTools Admin

1. Introduction

Welcome to EduGM UniTools. We provide educational calculators, academic utilities and PDF tools designed to support students and learners on our website and mobile app. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our services.

Privacy-first by design: Our PDF tools and academic calculators process your files and inputs locally on your device — inside your browser or app. Your PDFs, images and entered data are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include details you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, feedback or support messages. Technical information, such as browser type, device details and server logs, may also be processed by our hosting or technical service providers.

3. Information Entered into Tools — Processed Locally

Information entered into a calculator or uploaded into a PDF tool is processed directly in your browser or app. This includes:

  • PDF files you merge, split, compress, organize or convert.
  • Images you scan, compress or convert to PDF.
  • Marks, grades, percentages and other calculator inputs.

These files and inputs stay on your device and are not transmitted to our servers. Please still avoid entering passwords, banking details, government identification numbers or confidential information into general-purpose educational tools.

4. Camera and Device Permissions (Scan to PDF)

Some tools — such as Scan to PDF — let you capture pages using your device's camera or pick images from your gallery. To use these features, your browser or app may ask for permission to access:

  • Camera: to capture photos of documents for scanning.
  • Photo Gallery / Files: to select existing images to convert.

These permissions are requested only when you choose to use the feature. Images captured or selected are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. You can revoke these permissions at any time from your browser or app settings — doing so will only disable the camera/gallery features, not the rest of the website or app.

5. How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate educational and PDF tools.
  • To respond to enquiries and feedback.
  • To improve website and app performance and usability.
  • To troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To maintain website and app security and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

6. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website, app or their service providers may use cookies or similar technologies where enabled. Hosting, security, analytics or other third-party services may process technical information as needed to provide their services. Their own privacy policies may also apply. Third-party libraries loaded in your browser (such as PDF processing libraries) run locally and do not receive your files.

7. Data Sharing and Security

Because PDF files, images and calculator inputs are processed locally, they are not shared with us. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the website or app, or when disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law. We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Your Rights

Locally processed files and inputs are not retained by us after you close the tool or refresh the page. Any information you send us (such as support emails) may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose or as required by law. Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

EduGM UniTools provides educational resources that may be used by students. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and follow applicable requirements concerning children and parental consent.

10. External Links

Our website or app may link to external websites and educational resources. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Please review the relevant policies before providing information to an external service.

11. Educational Disclaimer

Our tools are intended to assist with academic calculations, document handling and general educational activities. Always verify important results with your university or another official source. EduGM UniTools should not be assumed to be affiliated with or endorsed by a university unless expressly authorized.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
