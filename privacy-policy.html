1. Introduction

Welcome to EduGM UniTools. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, stored, and protected when you visit or use our website, educational calculators, academic utilities, and other available services.

We understand that privacy is important to students, teachers, visitors, and other users. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and to explain our practices in a clear and simple way.

By accessing our website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will seek that consent separately when applicable.

This policy applies to the EduGM UniTools website and the educational services that link to this policy. It covers information collected through website visits, contact messages, and interactions with our tools, to the extent those activities involve information covered by applicable privacy laws.

This policy does not automatically apply to external websites, applications, or services operated by other organizations. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on the features you use and the technical configuration of our website. We may collect the following categories of information where applicable.

You may voluntarily provide information when contacting us, submitting feedback, reporting an issue, or requesting support. This information may include:

  • Your name, if you choose to provide it.
  • Your email address or other contact details.
  • The subject and content of your message.
  • Feedback, suggestions, or questions about our tools.
  • Other information you voluntarily include in your communication.

3.2 Technical Information

When you visit a website, the website server, hosting provider, or other enabled technical services may automatically process limited technical information, such as:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser name and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Pages requested and approximate access times.
  • Referring webpage, where available.
  • Technical error reports and basic diagnostic information.

Whether this information is recorded, and how long it is retained, depends on the services and server configuration in use.

4. Information Entered into Educational Tools

EduGM UniTools may provide academic calculators, percentage calculators, SGPA and CGPA calculators, engineering utilities, study resources, and other educational features.

Depending on the implementation of a particular tool, information entered by a user may be processed directly in the browser or transmitted to a server to provide the requested functionality. We will describe additional data practices where appropriate.

We do not claim that all information entered into every tool is automatically deleted or never stored. Data handling depends on the design and configuration of that tool and its supporting services.

Please do not enter passwords, banking details, government identification numbers, confidential examination information, or other sensitive personal data into general-purpose calculators.

Information may be used for the following purposes, where applicable:

  • To answer questions and respond to support requests.
  • To identify and resolve technical problems.

  • To understand website usage and improve user experience.
  • To detect abuse, spam, fraud, or unauthorized activity.

We aim to collect and use only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Where applicable privacy laws require a legal basis for processing personal information, we may rely on consent, providing a service requested by the user, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests such as website security and maintenance.

Essential cookies, where used, may support basic website functionality. Optional analytics or advertising technologies may be used only as permitted by applicable law and the relevant consent requirements.

You can review your browser settings to manage or delete cookies. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

This policy does not mean that every type of cookie is currently active on our website. Actual use depends on the services installed and enabled.
