Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to EduGM UniTools.

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EduGM UniTools. We provide educational calculators, academic utilities and other tools designed to support students and learners. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include details you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, feedback or support messages. Technical information, such as browser type, device details and server logs, may also be processed by our hosting or technical service providers.

3. Information Entered into Tools

Information entered into a calculator may be processed directly in your browser or sent to a server, depending on how that tool is implemented. Please avoid entering passwords, banking details, government identification numbers or confidential information into general-purpose educational tools.

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate educational tools.
  • To respond to enquiries and feedback.
  • To improve website performance and usability.
  • To troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies where enabled. Hosting, security, analytics or other third-party services may process technical information as needed to provide their services. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

6. Data Sharing and Security

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website, or when disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law. We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Your Rights

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose or as required by law. Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

EduGM UniTools provides educational resources that may be used by students. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and follow applicable requirements concerning children and parental consent.

9. External Links

Our website may link to external websites and educational resources. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Please review the relevant policies before providing information to an external service.

10. Educational Disclaimer

Our tools are intended to assist with academic calculations and general educational activities. Always verify important results with your university or another official source. EduGM UniTools should not be assumed to be affiliated with or endorsed by a university unless expressly authorized.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
