Percentage Calculation Formula

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example: If you score 425 out of 500:

(425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%

This calculator assumes that the marks obtained do not exceed the total maximum marks. It does not determine official pass/fail status or eligibility for scholarships.
