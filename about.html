Who We Are

More than a collection of calculators.

We believe useful digital products should make everyday tasks simpler, clearer and easier to access.

EduGM UniTools is an educational web-tools initiative created with students in mind. Its purpose is to bring helpful academic utilities together through a simple, organised and accessible online experience.

Students regularly work with calculations, grades, academic information and study-related tasks. Finding a suitable tool can sometimes mean visiting several websites or navigating complicated interfaces. EduGM UniTools aims to make that experience more convenient.

The idea is to create a place where students can discover useful tools without unnecessary complexity. Each tool should have a clear purpose, a straightforward interface and a design that works well on computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Good technology does not always need to be complicated. Sometimes, its greatest value is making something difficult feel simple.

EduGM UniTools is a developing initiative. Its features and direction may evolve as new ideas are explored, tools are developed and user needs become clearer.

Our Journey

From an engineering student's idea to a web project.

Every project has a beginning. Ours began with curiosity, the willingness to learn and the ambition to build.

During my first year of engineering, I became interested in building something useful beyond the classroom.

In 2026, I began exploring how web technologies could turn a simple idea into a working digital product. I wanted to learn about website design, coding, navigation and how different components could work together to create a useful experience.

Education felt like a natural place to start. Students often need practical tools for academic calculations and everyday study activities. This led to the idea of EduGM UniTools: a web-based platform focused on educational utilities.

Building a project also means dealing with real challenges. Layouts need to work on different screens, links must lead to the correct pages, and features should be tested rather than assumed to work. Each step offers another opportunity to learn.

The aim is not simply to put a name online, but to create a foundation that can be improved over time through practical development, careful testing and constructive feedback.

Our Approach

Built with curiosity. Improved through experience.

Every digital project requires more than a visual design. It needs a thoughtful structure, consistent navigation, careful testing and an understanding of the people who use it.

Our approach is to begin with a problem, consider a practical solution and build an interface that makes that solution understandable. The next steps are to test the experience, identify areas for improvement and refine the design.

We believe learning to build technology involves understanding both what works and what does not. Mistakes and technical challenges are part of the process, and each can provide useful experience when handled carefully.
