How to sign in

  1. Click the button above. A new tab opens with Zoho's official sign-in page.
  2. Enter your workspace email — for example yourname@mail.codebihin.in.
  3. Use your temporary password from the approval email sent by admin@codebihin.in.
  4. Set a new password when Zoho prompts you on first login.
  5. You're in. Your inbox is now ready to use.
