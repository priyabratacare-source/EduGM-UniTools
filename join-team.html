What happens next

  1. Our admin team reviews your registration within 1–2 business days.
  2. You'll receive an email from admin@codebihin.in confirming your mailbox is active.
  3. Check your spam / junk folder — first-time emails from us may land there. Mark it as "Not spam" so future messages arrive.
  4. The email will contain your workspace email (e.g. yourname@mail.codebihin.in), a temporary password, and sign-in instructions.
  5. Sign in at the mail portal, change your password, and start using your mailbox.
