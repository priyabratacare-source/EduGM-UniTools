Founder & Leadership

Priyabrata Patra

Founder · Chairman · CEO · Developer

Priyabrata Patra is the founder behind EduGM UniTools, an educational utility platform focused on making useful academic tools accessible through a simple and user-friendly online experience.

With an interest in technology, web development, and practical digital solutions, the goal is to build tools that help students and learners complete everyday academic tasks more conveniently.

From planning website features and shaping the user experience to developing and improving the platform, the focus is on usability, accessibility, and continuous improvement. EduGM UniTools is a growing project, with its features and direction evolving over time.

Priyabrata is also associated with CodeBihin, the technology brand connected with this project. The long-term aim is to develop useful digital products that solve real problems and provide a reliable experience for their users.
