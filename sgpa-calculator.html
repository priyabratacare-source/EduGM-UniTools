How to Calculate SGPA

2. Enter the corresponding grade point.
3. Click Calculate to view your SGPA.
4. Use Reset to start again.

This calculator uses a credit-weighted average. Check your university's official grading rules for special cases, audit courses, backlogs or non-credit subjects.
