Frequently asked questions

Will my PDF always become smaller?

Not always. Results depend on the document. Image-heavy PDFs often benefit most from lower image quality. Already optimized or text-heavy PDFs may become similar in size or larger.

Will the text remain selectable?

This version renders pages as images, so text is no longer selectable or searchable in the output. Use the original document when searchable text or accessibility is important.

Can I choose the download filename?

Yes. Edit the filename field before downloading. The tool automatically adds the .pdf extension.

Does this tool upload my document?

The PDF processing is performed in your browser. The PDF itself is not uploaded by this page. The PDF processing libraries are loaded from public CDN addresses.
