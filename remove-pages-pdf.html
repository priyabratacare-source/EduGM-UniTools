Frequently asked questions

Does my PDF get uploaded anywhere?

No. The entire process runs in your browser using PDF.js and pdf-lib. The file never leaves your device.

Can I keep only certain pages instead?

Yes — click “Select all” then un-select the pages you want to keep. The tool removes whatever is selected.

What happens to links, forms, and bookmarks?

The tool focuses on page content. Interactive elements like form fields and some annotations may not carry over to the rebuilt PDF. Text, images, and layout are preserved.

Can I remove all pages?

No — at least one page must remain. If you select every page, the Create button will be disabled.

How large can the file be?

Up to about 100 MB. Very large PDFs with many pages may take longer because each page thumbnail is rendered individually.
