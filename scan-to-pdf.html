Frequently asked questions

Do my images get uploaded anywhere?

No. Camera frames and uploaded images are processed entirely in your browser. They never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WEBP — any format your browser can display in an <img> tag works.

How do I get the best scan quality?

Use good lighting, keep the page flat and fill the frame. On phones, the rear camera produces sharper results. Images are placed at their native resolution unless the page size forces scaling.

Can I reorder the pages?

Pages are added in the order you capture or select them. To change the order, remove the images and re-add them in the desired sequence.

Why is the PDF page size important?

A4 and Letter create standard document pages. "Fit to image" makes each PDF page exactly the size of the image, which is useful for photos or scans of varying dimensions.
